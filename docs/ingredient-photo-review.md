# Ingredient-level photo review — proposed next iteration

Status: proposal, not a claim of implemented behavior.

Analyze visible foods individually, but group them within one meal. Show each estimated ingredient, quantity, protein, calories and uncertainty. Let users correct quantities, remove misidentified foods and add hidden ingredients such as oils, sauces and fillings. Confirm the complete meal once; edits recalculate its totals and daily totals.

For mixed dishes, offer a dish estimate or entry of a known recipe rather than inventing precise ingredient quantities. Preserve raw estimates separately from user corrections. AI guesses alone must not count as verified evidence for ingredient-variety badges.

Flow: photograph → review ingredients and uncertainty → correct → confirm meal → expandable ingredient breakdown in the journal.

Align photo and manual records with the planner's ingredient quantities. Review the existing draft/confirmation model before implementation; do not count ingredient rows as multiple meals or bypass confirmation.
